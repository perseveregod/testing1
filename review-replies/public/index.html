Questions

Good to know.

Does it post to Google or Yelp for me?

No. You copy the reply and paste it yourself, so you always get a final read before anything goes public.

Can I trust what the AI writes?

The drafts are usually ready to post, but AI can get things wrong, so always read before posting. ReplyDesk is told never to make up discounts, policies or promises you haven't given it.

Will every reply sound the same?

No. Each reply picks up specifics from that review, follows your chosen tone, and can be rewritten with one click.

What happens to my data?

Review text is sent to our AI provider only to write your reply. We don't sell data or run ad trackers, and you can export or delete everything from your account. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

I run a medical or dental practice. Anything special?

Yes. Never confirm that a reviewer is a patient or mention their care in a public reply. ReplyDesk drafts replies that move the conversation offline, but check every reply against your own privacy obligations.

How do I cancel?

Click Manage billing in the app and cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, and if you change your mind within 7 days of a charge, we'll refund it.
